## Pricing and service limits

Workspace onboarding requires no card and provides a one-time 1,000-response promotional grant to an eligible verified workspace. Accepted responses then consume prepaid credits at US$0.01 each; the minimum purchase is US$5 for 500 credits. There is no subscription, provisioning fee or monthly purchase commitment. See the [credit/refund policy](credits-and-refunds.md) for the acceptance unit and payment adjustments.

Collection limits, configured spending caps, expiry, revocation, rate controls and available balance may stop new acceptance. Identical retries return an existing eligible receipt without a second charge. A client reporting a network error should preserve the original submission and retry key; changing the key can create a distinct response.

The intended retention defaults are 90-day raw answers, 24-hour export download availability and seven-day rolling backups, with the qualifications in the privacy notice.
