# L14 activation and preview learning plan

Prepared 2026-09-10. **Protocol only: no participants recruited, sessions run, adoption measured, or new analytics installed by this work.** The companion `ACTIVATION-TRACKING.csv` contains headers only. L14 remains open until consented observations and evidence exist.

The decision is whether independent developers can obtain value from the documented API/MCP/CLI and customer-owned web/mobile integration, and which friction to fix before broader acquisition. This plan adds no survey distribution, GUI builder or analytics product requirement. Product owns the review; marketing owns audience learning; engineering validates operational evidence; the founder authorizes outreach; the privacy/data owner approves collection and retention. These are role assignments, not approvals already received.

## Keep the cohorts distinct

1. **Usability study:** three independent developers who have not built Likerts. Use controlled synthetic responses in disposable study workspaces. Report first-use completion, assistance and friction as study results. These are not organic adoption or real respondent volume.
2. **Design partners:** target five relevant independent organizations with a real post-action web/mobile feedback need. A person may participate in both tracks, but label overlap; do not count one organization twice. Participation and stated willingness to pay do not equal paid conversion.
3. **Product adoption:** eligible external workspaces using the product for their own use case, after consent/privacy and preview gates. Exclude staff, founder, agents, automated checks, demonstrations, usability fixtures, payment acceptance transactions and all other synthetic workspaces before computing **both numerators and denominators**. Exclude `hosted-launch-*` explicitly: the sustained-rehearsal runner restricts its synthetic workspaces to that prefix. Prefix filtering alone is insufficient; staff browser workspaces use ordinary `ws_…` IDs. Unknown classification is reported separately and excluded pending review.

Use one canonical workspace per organization/use case for this small study, assigned before results are known. Do not retroactively merge or exclude failing workspaces to improve rates. Retain withdrawal/deletion counts in the cohort accounting where permitted; suppress individual data according to the approved policy. Explain any resulting missing observations. Do not infer external use from `actor_kind=human` or production hostname alone.

## Definitions and denominators

All windows are exact elapsed time in UTC; `7d = 168h`, `30d = 720h`. Freeze a report cutoff `C` and record it. Cohort time `t0` is the first authorized browser workspace/membership creation for a new eligible external workspace, **not** rendering the sign-in page. Existing accounts/workspaces are a separate returning cohort. A workspace can reach each milestone once; repeated credentials, responses and checkouts do not create more activated workspaces.

Three primary KPIs are enough initially:

| KPI | Definition and denominator | Decision/caveat |
|---|---|---|
| 7-day activation | Eligible workspaces with a first accepted response in `[t0,t0+7d)` / all eligible workspaces whose `t0+7d <= C`. | Diagnose failure to reach collection value. Include observed zero-response workspaces; report immature workspaces separately, not as failures or successes. A published collection or client callback alone does not count. |
| 7-day repeat collection | Activated eligible workspaces with another distinct accepted response in `[first_response+24h, first_response+7d)` / all activated eligible workspaces with `first_response+7d <= C`. | Measures collection continuing after first use; it is not proof of owner return or respondent retention. Exclude idempotent retries and synthetic bursts. Report owner return only from a separately evidenced management action. |
| 30-day live paid conversion | Eligible new workspaces with a reconciled, positive live purchase by `t0+30d` / all eligible new workspaces with `t0+30d <= C`. | Separate all-workspace conversion from the diagnostic activated-to-paid rate (denominator: activated workspaces whose own `first_response+30d <= C`, numerator: live purchase in that window). Test payments, free grants and manually issued credits never count. Show refunds/disputes separately and label gross versus net; a checkout return URL is not payment evidence. |

For early small cohorts always show `n/N` and observation dates, not a percentage alone. No numeric adoption/retention/conversion target is justified yet. Three usability completions and five partner discussions are learning targets specified in L14, not market demand evidence. Do not claim trends or statistical significance from them.

Use these diagnostics to locate the problem; do not mix their denominators:

| Stage/driver | Definition and denominator | Current evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Visit → docs | Same-window unique consenting sessions that view docs / eligible site sessions, only if existing provider definitions support this. | Provider aggregate pageviews cannot be joined to accounts or treated as unique people. If unavailable, report separate aggregates or **unmeasured**. |
| Invitation/authentication | Observed participants who finish real sign-in / invited, consented participants who start it. Report declined invitations and blocked access separately. | Invitation-only preview is not an open-signup funnel. Account creation needs Clerk/provider or observed identity proof; a rendered modal proves neither signup nor verification. |
| Authentication → workspace | Participants with authorized workspace bootstrap / participants with confirmed real sign-in. | Workspace/membership creation can evidence bootstrap; it cannot reconstruct failed logins. |
| Workspace → credential | Eligible mature workspaces with a service credential within 7d of `t0` / the same mature `t0` cohort. | Creating a credential does not prove it was copied, saved or used. Record one successful scoped management request separately in observed sessions. |
| Credential → accepted response | Credentialed workspaces whose first accepted response occurs within 7d after their first credential / credentialed workspaces with that full window observed. | This denominator uses credential time, so it differs from the primary workspace cohort. Survey create/publish and collection-create timestamps explain intermediate drop-offs. |
| Time to first value | Wall-clock time from observed docs start to first server-accepted response; also measure bootstrap-to-response. Report median/range among completers plus completion `n/N`, censored/blocked counts and support minutes for everyone. | Do not delete waiting or failed sessions, or calculate a fast median only without reporting non-completion. A separate retrieval/export check completes the study journey. |
| Checkout settlement | Unique live checkouts with matching settled purchase credit / unique live checkouts started, grouped by checkout-created cohort; show pending/open/failed/expired separately at cutoff. | Reused idempotency keys are one checkout. Current DB does not store a live/test flag: provider `livemode` or an independently verified environment record is mandatory before classifying payment. |

Guardrails: **(1)** privacy/security incidents, wrong-workspace access or missing consent stop that session and escalate; report count, no payload. **(2)** support burden and collection correctness: total intervention minutes per starter, failed/ambiguous acceptance counts, and duplicate receipt/ledger mismatches. Any duplicate debit or false success is a release defect even if activation looks good. Do not force top-ups merely to improve conversion or consume all 1,000 promotional credits during a study.

## Three-developer first-use protocol

Use a 30–45 minute observation slot as a scheduling assumption, not a success threshold. Participants use their own permitted development environment and a disposable synthetic fixture. Seek different entry preferences across API, CLI and an accepted agent setup; let participants choose a relevant released SDK. This study does not replace five-SDK technical acceptance.

1. Explain preview limits, purpose, voluntary participation, what is recorded, approved retention and withdrawal route. Obtain consent before observation. Recording/screenshots need separate explicit consent; by default use redacted notes. Tell participants to stop screen sharing while handling OTP, passwords/passkeys, secret tokens or payment details. They authenticate themselves; observers never request those secrets.
2. Start at the public site/docs. Ask the participant to explain what Likerts does, its pricing unit, and who owns survey distribution. Record misunderstandings without coaching. Time docs start and the first blocker.
3. Have them complete authorized sign-in and workspace onboarding, choose/create a least-privilege expiring credential, save it privately, and configure their selected API/CLI/agent client. Confirm a workspace-scoped read works. Record copy/setup/recovery friction without storing credential values. Do not represent unaccepted OAuth as available.
4. From docs, create and publish a small survey, create a bounded collection, integrate the chosen released SDK in a local host, then submit a synthetic answer. Verify the server receipt, distinct response ID and one corresponding usage/credit consumption. Retry the same intended submission to check idempotence without charging twice. A UI callback or capabilities declaration is insufficient.
5. Retrieve the accepted response or generate and open an authorized export; inspect balance and understand the blocking reason/threshold notice. Explain top-up and cancellation using approved sandbox evidence if desired. **No real purchase in this study without separate live-payment authority; sandbox success is excluded from conversion.** Revoke the test credential and verify denial. Clean up disposable resources through accepted paths; record what was actually deleted versus retained under policy.
6. Remain silent initially. If they are blocked and choose assistance, record timestamp, blocker, intervention and minutes; label completion `assisted`. A clear explanation requiring no operational intervention can be recorded separately as a clarification. Do not quietly fix credentials/configuration for them and call it independent completion.
7. End with what they expected, hardest step, whether they could repeat it tomorrow and one priority change. Record short redacted summaries, not customer data. At the consented seven-day follow-up, distinguish an intention to return, a fresh synthetic rerun, and verified real use. No automated reminder/outreach is created by this document.

Per-person outcome is `completed_unassisted`, `completed_assisted`, `blocked`, `withdrew` or `not_observed`. Report all starts and replacements; never replace a failed participant without retaining the failed observation in the summary. L14's three-completion target is unmet while fewer than three independent developers complete documented first use; report assistance openly and fix recurring blockers before repeating.

## Five-design-partner protocol

Recruit only after the outreach gate. Prefer developers/product owners with an existing web/mobile action where a short embedded question has a clear purpose (for example checkout completion or support resolution). Capture broad platform and use-case categories, not confidential workflows. Include prospective buyers capable of evaluating integration effort and usage cost; do not infer enterprise needs or require a builder.

Discuss their current workaround, trigger/eligibility, required question families, response volume range, data isolation/deletion requirements, installation/control-surface preference and who approves spend. Ask them to react to **1,000 free accepted responses and US$0.01 per additional accepted response**, with any currently approved top-up/preview conditions. Ask what observable value would justify paying and what would prevent it. Record `would_not_pay`, `conditional_interest`, `verbal_intent`, `pilot_agreed`, or `verified_live_purchase` only with corresponding evidence; do not turn verbal intent into revenue. Do not promise enterprise features, SLAs or support coverage beyond current approved terms.

Each discussion ends with a participant-approved next step or none. Quotes, logos and case studies require separate publication permission. Summarize repeated objections and contrasting evidence across five organizations; avoid averaging hypothetical willingness-to-pay into a forecast. Revise positioning or onboarding before broad paid traffic. Record support/infrastructure economics separately from this small cohort.

## Tracking and review

The blank CSV is a **private working template** when populated, not an invitation to commit participant data. Copy it to the privacy-owner-approved restricted location first. One row represents one participant/workspace study pairing. `study_id`/`organization_study_id`/`workspace_study_id` are random aliases; the mapping stays separate. Enumerated `track` is `usability`, `design_partner` or `adoption`; use a second linked row for overlap. `exclusion_reason` distinguishes staff/automation/hosted_launch/usability_fixture/demo/payment_acceptance/unknown. Adoption-eligible rows require approved classification; empty is unknown, not false or zero.

Use ISO8601 UTC timestamps, numeric elapsed/support minutes, and the exact statuses above. Leave unobserved fields blank and explain an error code/short category. Evidence references point to access-controlled redacted records, never secret-bearing URLs. For an observation at cutoff `C`, a missing event with a fully observed window is a non-conversion; an immature, withdrawn or inaccessible window is reported with that status, not silently treated as zero. Do not backfill from memory.

After each session Product triages blockers with an owner and acceptance criterion. Review weekly once eligible rows exist: cohort sizes/exclusions, stage `n/N`, timing and assistance, top two repeated blockers, any privacy/accounting incidents, partner objections, and one concrete next change. Finance verifies live conversion classification. Privacy owner approves deletion of study notes/mapping on the recorded date; engineering preserves only records required by the separate approved operational/financial retention policy. No raw study result is made public without review.

L14 evidence is complete only when: privacy/outreach gates are recorded; sources and exclusion register are verified; three actual independent first-use completions and every non-completion are documented; five partner observations (or the explicit remaining gap) are reported; matured seven-day and paid windows are distinguished from still-pending observations; and the resulting product/marketing decisions are recorded. Preparing this plan does not close those gates.
