# Pricing

Likerts Community Edition is free and open source under the MIT License.

- No response credits
- No per-response charge
- No subscription or checkout
- No license key
- No application-level response quota

Self-hosting still has infrastructure costs. Database storage, compute, object storage, network traffic, email identity and observability are purchased directly from the providers you choose.

Operational protections remain configurable. Request-rate limits, payload bounds, export bounds, offline-queue capacity and worker concurrency protect a deployment; they are not commercial entitlements or billing meters.
