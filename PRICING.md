# Pricing and free onboarding

Likerts launches with one simple commercial path:

- Creating a workspace is free and requires no payment method.
- Each verified workspace receives one nonrenewing grant of **1,000 accepted responses**.
- After that grant is consumed, each accepted response costs **US$0.01**.
- The smallest paid credit purchase is **US$5**, which adds 500 response credits. This is purchased usage, not a setup fee, subscription or minimum monthly spend.
- Invalid submissions, rejected submissions and identical idempotent retries consume no free or paid credit.

The free grant belongs to the workspace billing account. It cannot be transferred, withdrawn, regenerated by deleting a workspace or multiplied through repeated signup. A verified owner may begin without a card. At exhaustion, reads, exports and valid prior receipts continue, while new responses pause until paid credit is available.

## Acceptance and ledger order

The ledger remains the source of truth. Within one database transaction, a new valid submission locks the workspace billing account, consumes one promotional credit when available or one paid response credit otherwise, then commits the response and its unique usage entry. The receipt records the funding class without changing the public one-response unit. Concurrent attempts cannot consume the final credit twice. A retried accepted response returns its original receipt before the balance check.

Use append-only entries for promotional grants, purchases, response consumption, refunds, reversals and administrative corrections. Never edit or delete historical usage to change a balance. Promotional credits have no cash value and refunds must never convert them into paid credits.

## Customer experience

Usage surfaces must show free credits remaining, paid credits remaining, lifetime accepted responses, the current blocking reason and low-balance thresholds. Notify the workspace owner at 80%, 90% and 100% of the promotional grant and paid balance. Payment setup and purchase are explicit browser handoffs that API, MCP and CLI can initiate and inspect.

Do not silently charge, enable debt or accept a response beyond the available balance. Automatic top-up may be added only as an explicit owner-controlled setting with an amount, trigger and spending cap.

## Abuse boundary

The grant requires a verified owner and is issued once by an idempotent server operation. Rate limits, collection caps and global abuse controls apply before expensive work. Detection may use bounded account, payment and traffic signals, but IP address alone is not an identity boundary. Suspicious workspaces may be held before grant issuance or paid activation without exposing the signals used.

Before launch, measure activation, grant consumption, conversion, abandoned credits, duplicate-workspace abuse, processor fees and infrastructure cost. The older AWS shared-fleet snapshot predates the promotional grant and callback worker. The separate [Render + Vercel + Clerk launch model](economics/LAUNCH-PLATFORM-ECONOMICS.md) includes both as explicit planning assumptions, but hosted bills, capacity, support and abuse evidence are still required before claiming margin.
