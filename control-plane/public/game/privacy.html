Privacy in Likerts.

Local play stays on your device. Sharing a challenge or joining a live room sends the information needed for that choice. Here is what travels, who can see it and how to remove it.

Effective 7 November 2026. This policy covers the Likerts browser game, website, iPhone app and native Messages extension. Publisher: CROSSTABS PTY LTD. Privacy contact: support@crosstabs.com.

No game account is required. Likerts does not include advertising or analytics SDKs in the app or these game pages. Optional live play uses our game server; it is different from offline play and local practice.

Use a nickname. Do not put contact details, sensitive personal information or someone else’s private messages into a quiz, answer or challenge.

Games and quizzes on your device

The iPhone app can store settings, topic history, Daily best information, hidden-challenge fingerprints and an unfinished standalone game on your device. A saved game includes its nickname, statements, answers, guesses and results. Pass-the-phone party boards are temporary and do not sync to an online leaderboard.

Quizzes you create or import are saved in that app or browser, including questions, answer choices, correct answers and explanations. The library does not automatically upload or sync. Practice takes place locally and does not upload your answers to our game server. Quiz export files include the answer key; you control where exported copies go.

The browser uses local storage for its quiz library and tab storage for live-room access and interrupted hosting requests. A pending hosting request can contain a copy of the quiz and its answer key. Clearing browser storage removes those local copies and access, but does not close a server room or remove exported files.

On iPhone, the standalone save and quiz library use operating-system file protection, are not separately encrypted by Likerts and are excluded from device backup when saved. Other preferences and existing system backups follow your device’s settings. Use the quiz library’s Delete control to remove a saved quiz. Leaving a standalone game attempts to remove its saved game; a storage error is shown if removal fails. Offloading the app may preserve data.

Challenge codes and Messages

Offline challenge codes and Messages cards contain nicknames, statements and answers. Results can include names, scores and answer comparisons. These payloads are encoded, not encrypted or tamper-proof by Likerts. Anyone who receives the content may be able to read or copy it.

Copying puts the selected content on the system clipboard. Sharing uses the app or communication service you choose and its privacy rules. Adding a Messages card stages an unsent draft; you choose whether to send it. Apple manages the conversation and its copies separately from Likerts’ local saves. See Apple’s privacy information.

Hiding a received challenge clears its local progress when storage permits and saves a local fingerprint. Settings can clear the hidden list; Messages maintains a separate list. Hiding does not send a report, block a sender or erase a card from a conversation.

Optional live rooms

Hosting or joining live play sends information to the Likerts game server to admit players, keep everyone in the same game, score rounds, recover interrupted requests and prevent abuse. This includes room settings and codes, nicknames, assigned player identifiers and avatars, connection status, answers or predictions, and scores.

Hosting a quiz uploads a fixed copy of that quiz, including its correct answers and explanations. The app asks before uploading it. Your other saved quizzes do not automatically upload. Players confirm room sharing before joining.

Nicknames and presence appear in the lobby. An individual player’s submitted answer remains private from other participants until reveal. At reveal, the room can see the submitted answer or prediction, relevant answer comparisons and points. For quizzes, the revealed correct answer and explanation are shown too. Other players can remember, screenshot or copy what they see.

Anyone with a room code can join an unlocked lobby, subject to its limits. The host can lock the room or remove players. Invite links contain a room code, not a member’s private access credential or the quiz’s answer key. Share codes only with your intended players.

Saved access lets you reconnect. On iPhone, scoped room access and a pending upload-recovery copy use device-only Keychain storage; the app attempts to remove a pending copy when admission is resolved or explicitly forgotten, and shows a retry notice if secure-storage removal fails. Returning Home keeps your place. Forgetting saved access removes that device’s recovery information and does not itself close the server room.

Leaving, closing and retention

Leave removes your current seat and revokes its access. It does not erase earlier revealed answers, your nickname or scores from the room’s existing results. The host’s Close room ends access for everyone and removes active room content from the application’s database.

The application expires active rooms after a maximum of six hours, two hours idle, or fifteen minutes after finishing, whichever comes first. Expired access is rejected; physical cleanup may happen later. Admission recovery has a separate ten-minute window. Limited recovery and terminal records, including generated identifiers, hashes, a room code and timestamps, expire within six hours; physical cleanup can happen later.

These application limits do not promise immediate deletion from provider logs and backups, database history, device backups, exported files, Messages conversations or copies retained by other people. Those copies have separate retention and controls. Close room does not erase them all.

Use Leave or Close when finished, delete quizzes you no longer need, and manage exported files, browser storage and device backups separately. Contact us about information held by the service; do not post private access credentials or other people’s answers publicly.

Hosting and network information

The marketing website runs on Vercel. Live game traffic goes to Render, and live rooms are stored in a dedicated PostgreSQL database. The game service is currently hosted in Singapore. These providers operate infrastructure in multiple countries, so processing and support can involve countries other than yours.

Network requests expose information needed to deliver and secure the service, such as an IP address, request time, requested URL, browser or connection details and error information. The application processes addresses for abuse controls and stores hashed rate-limit keys. Infrastructure providers can process request information in their own logs. A room code in an invite URL can appear in request records, so treat it as an invitation rather than a secret password.

Connections to the live service use HTTPS. Access credentials are scoped to a room; the server stores token verifiers. No system can prevent a participant from copying revealed content. There are no advertising or analytics scripts in these game pages, but hosting requests are still processed.

Provider logs and backups are separate from room expiry; this policy does not set a fixed deletion schedule for those provider copies. See Vercel’s privacy notice and Render’s privacy policy. Contact us for questions about the information held for this game.

Optional on-device AI

Handpicked themed games remain available without AI. On supported Apple Intelligence devices, optional AI selects from reviewed English statements using the theme and reviewed menu. It does not receive your nickname or answers. You can review, discard or cancel the selection.

Likerts does not send this game content to a remote AI API. Apple manages the system model’s availability and downloads. This feature is separate from uploading a quiz to a live room.

Support and privacy requests

The app’s support screen prepares only the text you write and the app version/build. Preparing or copying a draft does not deliver a report to CROSSTABS. Opening it in an email app passes the text to that app; an email provider may sync unsent drafts. It does not automatically attach game content, conversation identifiers, logs, screenshots or device identifiers.

If you choose Send in an email app, your sender details, the message and anything you add go to CROSSTABS PTY LTD at support@crosstabs.com and are handled by email providers. Your email app may add a display name, address or signature. Correspondence is separate from room data and is not deleted by closing a room.

Write to that address for game help, a content concern, or a request to access, correct or delete information we hold. Explain what you are asking about without sending a private access credential or unnecessary information about other people. We may need enough information to locate the relevant record and verify the request. Applicable rights and any legally required retention depend on your circumstances.

Local-only information is controlled on your device rather than through a game account. You can choose offline play, decline live-room sharing, leave a room, close a room you host, delete local quizzes, clear a hidden list, and manage copies through the service used to share them. Support explains these controls.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date when these practices change. Check the current policy before choosing a feature that sends information beyond your device.
