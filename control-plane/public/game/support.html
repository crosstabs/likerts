A few useful answers.

How does the iPhone quick game start?

Choose Start a quick game, rate five statements from Not me to So me, then pass the phone. Your friend locks each prediction before seeing the answer. Exact reads earn 100 points; each step away costs 25. The final percentage is the average of the five rounds.

You can choose from 20 topics and 1,000 illustrated takes, or use a handpicked Fresh Takes theme. The interface and game writing are in English.

How do I start or reconnect to a live room?

Open Play Live to host or join by the six-character room code. Use a nickname and confirm sharing before joining. Keep the same browser tab to resume saved access. A room code is different from the encoded challenge codes used by the native app.

A host can close a room; a player can leave it. Hosts upload a fixed quiz copy with its answer key. Answers are private until reveal and then visible to the room. Use Quizzes to create or practice locally. Quiz export files include their correct answers.

Why will a challenge code not open?

Both players need the Likerts iOS app. From Home, choose Enter a challenge and paste the complete code. A malformed, unsupported or locally hidden code produces an explanation. Ask your friend for a new code if needed.

A code contains the nickname, takes and answers. It is encoded, not encrypted. Do not include the whole code or someone else's answers in a support message unless you deliberately choose to share them.

How do Messages games work?

Open Likerts in a one-to-one Messages conversation with one friend. Group or unavailable conversations cannot accept a game card. Adding a challenge stages an unsent card; you choose when to tap Send. Both players need Likerts and its Messages extension.

A local preview is not evidence of delivery. If adding fails, check the active conversation and retry from the visible game. Messages stores and manages its cards and conversations separately from Likerts' standalone save.

What does optional AI do?

Handpicked themed packs are available without it. On a compatible Apple Intelligence device running iOS 26 or later, an available system model can select and order statements from reviewed English writing. It receives the theme and reviewed menu, not your name or answers.

Review all five before playing. You can cancel, discard a preview or use handpicked takes. Unavailable, invalid or timed-out selection has a labelled handpicked route. Apple controls model downloads and availability.

What are Daily Read and party play?

Daily Read is a solo puzzle about an authored fictional character. Seven characters rotate at midnight UTC. Use their clues to predict five answers. A game already in progress keeps its original date.

Party play lets a host answer once and friends take turns on the same phone. Only completed turns appear on the local board. Replaying a friend's turn replaces that friend's score, and ties share a rank. Party boards are temporary and do not form an online leaderboard.

How do I control sound or clear progress?

Settings offers independent music, sound-effect and haptic controls. The main app is designed to respect Silent mode; its native Messages game remains silent.

Leaving a standalone game or returning home marks its save as abandoned and attempts to remove it. If file removal fails, Likerts warns you and does not silently restore the abandoned game; a local file can remain. Other settings, topic history and Daily best information may remain under the device's normal app-data controls. Offloading may preserve data.

The standalone save directory is excluded from backup when saving. Other preferences and existing system backups follow the operating system's behaviour. See the privacy basics.

What goes into a local support draft?

The in-app draft contains only the app version/build and the text you choose to write. It does not automatically add question identifiers, game nicknames, takes, answers, full challenge codes, conversation/session identifiers, logs, screenshots, attachments or device identifiers.

A local draft is not a delivered report. The recipient is support@crosstabs.com. Review the recipient and the whole message before choosing Send in your email app. This page only supplies the email subject; it does not read your game or prefill private content. Your email app may add your sender address, display name, signature or other details. If you have a question about your correspondence, write to this address and explain your request.

Does hiding send a report or block someone?

No. Hiding clears the challenge's local progress and adds its content fingerprint to the local hidden list. Settings can clear that list, and Messages maintains a separate list. Hiding does not send a report, erase a Messages card or create an authenticated sender block.

Use the communication service's conversation and sender controls as appropriate. Do not assume that preparing, copying or sharing a local draft delivered it.
