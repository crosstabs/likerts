A few good things to know

Likerts is an iPhone game with a native Messages extension, including extension use on iPad. App Store launch is on the way. This page shows the real app; it is not a playable browser game.

Handpicked Fresh Takes remain available without AI. Optional on-device AI selects reviewed English writing, with a five-take preview to accept or discard. It requires compatible Apple Intelligence hardware, iOS 26 or later and an available system model. It does not generate unlimited free-form questions.

Local party play lets friends take turns on one phone. Share codes and Messages cards include nicknames, takes and answers; they are encoded, not encrypted by Likerts. Use a service you trust and share only with your intended recipient.

Hiding a received challenge is a local control. It does not deliver a report, erase a Messages card or block a sender. Get help or prepare a content-report email.

Privacy basics

Likerts has no game account, app-operated game server, advertising or analytics SDK. Games and settings are kept locally. An unfinished standalone game can be saved on your device; Settings explains storage and deletion.

A challenge code or Messages card contains the nickname, takes and answers needed to play. Copying uses the system clipboard, and sharing uses the service you choose. Optional AI processing is on device.

A support draft includes only the text you write and the app version/build. The email app adds sender details. Nothing from your game or conversations is attached automatically. Preparing a draft sends nothing; you choose whether to send it.

These game pages have no login, forms or analytics scripts. Our website host, Vercel, can process request information such as IP address, browser details, requested URL and time to deliver and secure the site. The existing survey product at this domain’s root has separate services and data flows.

For game help, content concerns or privacy questions, visit Likerts support or write to support@crosstabs.com. Please leave out private codes, answers and other people’s messages.
