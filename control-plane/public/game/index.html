This page introduces the Likerts iPhone game. The existing open-source survey product, its documentation, services and data practices remain separate.

Go with your instinct.

1. Pick your takes.

Rate five statements from 1, “Not me,” to 5, “So me.” Explore 20 topics and 1,000 illustrated takes.

2. Pass the phone.

Your friend predicts each answer, locks the guess and gets the reveal. Local party play lets more friends take turns.

3. Find your wavelength.

See where your instincts matched. Share a code or play a one-to-one Messages challenge with another person who has Likerts.

Play it your way.

Daily Read introduces authored fictional characters. Handpicked Fresh Takes work without the optional model. On supported Apple Intelligence devices, on-device AI can select reviewed English writing; every pack is available to preview or discard. It does not create unlimited new free-form questions.

Optional AI picks require compatible hardware, iOS 26 or later and an available system model. Handpicked play remains available. Codes and Messages cards include nicknames, takes and answers; they are encoded, not encrypted. Both players need Likerts installed.

Your takes. Your choice to share.

Likerts has no game account, app-operated game server, advertising or analytics SDK. Games and settings are kept locally. An unfinished standalone game can be saved on your device; the app explains storage and deletion in Settings.

A challenge code or Messages card contains the nickname, takes and answers needed to play. It is encoded, not encrypted by Likerts. Copying uses the system clipboard, and sharing uses the service you choose. Hide a received challenge locally or contact us about a concern; hiding does not send a report or block its sender.

The game pages are static and have no login, forms or analytics scripts. Our website host, Vercel, can process request information such as IP address, browser details, requested URL and time to deliver and secure the site. The survey product at this domain’s root has separate services and data flows.
