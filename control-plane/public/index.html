What buyers ask first.

What counts as a response?

One accepted survey submission, including its answers. It is not a charge per question. Rejected submissions and identical retries with the same idempotency key consume no additional credit.

Is the free grant monthly?

No. Each eligible verified workspace gets one nonrenewing grant of 1,000 accepted responses. No card is required for the grant.

What happens when credits run out?

New responses pause. Authorized reads, exports and valid prior receipts remain available, subject to retention and access controls. There is no automatic top-up or silent charge.

Can I buy credits today?

Payments are in test mode during this developer preview. Planned paid usage is US$0.01 per accepted response, prepaid in packs starting at US$5. Live payment availability and reviewed credit, refund and tax terms will be published before paid launch.

Does Likerts send surveys to my customers?

Your application decides when, where and to whom a survey appears. Likerts supplies collection tools; your app handles eligibility, consent and distribution.
