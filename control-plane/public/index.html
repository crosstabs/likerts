A few good things to know

Play Live hosts shared mind-reading games and quizzes in a browser. A separate iPhone game with a native Messages extension, including extension use on iPad, is coming to the App Store. The app screenshots above show that native game.

Handpicked Fresh Takes remain available without AI. Optional on-device AI selects reviewed English writing, with a five-take preview to accept or discard. It requires compatible Apple Intelligence hardware, iOS 26 or later and an available system model. It does not generate unlimited free-form questions.

Local party play lets friends take turns on one phone. Share codes and Messages cards include nicknames, takes and answers; they are encoded, not encrypted by Likerts. Use a service you trust and share only with your intended recipient.

Hiding a received challenge is a local control. It does not deliver a report, erase a Messages card or block a sender. Get help or prepare a content-report email.

Privacy basics

No game account is required. Live games use our game server: it receives room codes, nicknames, answers and guesses so everyone sees the same game. Hosts upload a fixed copy of a hosted quiz, including its answer key. Answers remain private to their player until reveal, then the room can see them. Use a nickname and share the room code only with your intended players.

Your browser stores room access on that tab to support reconnecting, plus your quiz library on that browser. Quiz exports include correct answers. Practice stays local. The native app keeps offline games and settings locally; challenge codes and Messages cards contain the nickname, takes and answers needed to play. Sharing uses the service you choose. Optional native AI processing is on device.

A support draft includes only the text you write and the app version/build. The email app adds sender details. Nothing from your game or conversations is attached automatically. Preparing a draft does not deliver a report to CROSSTABS. Opening it passes the text to your email app, whose provider may sync unsent drafts; you choose whether to send it.

The marketing site runs on Vercel; live games run on Render with a PostgreSQL database. These providers can process request information such as IP address, browser details, requested URL and time to deliver and secure the service. There are no advertising or analytics scripts in these game pages. Active room content has application limits of six hours, two hours idle, or fifteen minutes after finishing; admission recovery has a separate ten-minute window. Provider logs and backups have separate retention. Other players can retain what they have seen or copied.

For game help, content concerns or privacy questions, visit Likerts support or write to support@crosstabs.com. Please leave out private codes, answers and other people’s messages.
