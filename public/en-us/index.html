Likerts helps researchers turn a defined audience, decision, and assumptions into directional synthetic hypotheses—then prepare the human research needed to test them.

Research boundary: Synthetic output is model-generated, not a survey of people. It is not representative, deterministic, causal evidence, or a substitute for human validation.
