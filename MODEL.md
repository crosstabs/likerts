# Product model

Likerts is collection infrastructure for surveys embedded in customer-controlled web and mobile experiences.

The product owns four things:

1. A bounded, portable survey schema.
2. Secure publication and collection credentials.
3. Idempotent validation and storage of responses.
4. The same management capabilities through API, MCP, and CLI.

Likerts does not send email, distribute public survey links, select respondents, or own the surrounding customer journey. Host applications decide when to present a survey and what approved metadata to attach.

The software is free under the MIT license. Accepted-response counts are operational telemetry. Operators pay their own infrastructure provider and configure the safety controls appropriate to their deployment.
